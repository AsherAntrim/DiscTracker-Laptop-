Disc Golf Catalog App

This is a SwiftUI-based iOS app that allows users to catalog their disc golf discs. Users can add, view, edit, and delete discs from their collection. The app supports adding custom images for each disc and allows sorting the collection by disc attributes like name, type, plastic, and condition.

Features

Add Discs: Users can add new discs to their catalog, including custom information such as the disc's name, type, plastic, condition, and an image.
View Disc Details: Tapping on a disc in the list will take users to a detailed view that shows more information about the disc.
Sort Discs: Discs can be sorted by different attributes, such as name, type, plastic, and condition.
Delete Discs: Swipe left on a disc in the list to delete it from the catalog.
Persistent Storage: Discs are saved to UserDefaults, ensuring the data is stored locally and persists across app restarts.
Dark Mode: The app supports dark mode with custom colors for background and text.
Screenshots

Adding a Disc

Tap the "+" button in the top-right corner of the app to add a new disc.
Enter the disc's name, type, plastic, and condition in the form.
Optionally, tap the "Select Image" button to upload an image of the disc from your photo library.
Tap the "Add Disc" button to save the disc to your catalog.
Viewing Disc Details
Tap on any disc in the list to view detailed information about the disc.
The detailed view shows the disc’s name, type, plastic, condition, and image.
Deleting a Disc
Swipe left on any disc in the list to reveal the delete button.
Tap the delete button to remove the disc from your catalog.
Sorting Discs
Use the segmented control at the top of the screen to sort discs by different attributes: name, type, plastic, and condition.
